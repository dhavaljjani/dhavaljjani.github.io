A medieval fantasy pub golf on Castro Street in MV

A short and quick post

Originally posted on substack on May 26, 2026

I’m not a particularly social or naturally extroverted person. I’ve had to get there manually. Some people tell me I just am, but that’s kinda funny right? They’re the ones actually talking and telling me that. I’m the one listening. I don’t think I’m social or extroverted because I end up being the guy listening often. Which I do enjoy quite a bit. But I have to egg myself on, in a sense, to actively try and be that person who can start things. I have to trick myself. That’s what I did with this. Once I put the flyers up, I had to do something. If not, someone more than just myself would be disappointed.

This idea’s been knocking around my mind for almost a year now. How fun would it be to get a ragtag group of people out walking place to place wearing costumes and getting drunk. I’m a child because I love the idea so much. This was the course:

It’s specifically a Mountain View pub golf. What is a pub golf you ask? A bar crawl dressed up as an elaborate drinking game (the British I tell you). It’s golf but with alcohol. Nine bars, instead of nine holes. And each hole has a drink which has a par score. You have to get your, let’s say pint of lager, down in three sips. If you get it down in 5, thats a +2. It was a glorious drunk day. Lots of people came from my flyers, some friends came from my life. Lots of costumes and energy and excitement. We ended with karaoke at Savvy Cellar, which happens every Saturday and I totally recommend checking it out to readers. Their karaoke is unique in that there’s no stage, and instead people can grab the wireless mics and sing with friends wherever they’d like. A delight.

Hopefully some connections that were made will last longer. It was a bit of effort by me, and I’m so glad it paid off. I spent time talking to all the bars about a larger group, I made some trivia questions, tried to explain the rules many times. I have learned that while Paul Bunyan is folklore, you cannot consider him fantasy or else people will have their pitchforks out. In jest of course.

I’ve kept this short. Sometimes I want to keep a memory to myself, without putting it on (digital) paper. I think I will keep this one in my mind. A day I felt like I won. I hope some people reading this find some use out of that map. Go get crazy out there. All while staying safe of course. Maybe join us next time, I’ll post about it before it happens on substack again, like I did with this one. Already made a course for Campbell, but maybe also Willow Glen, maybe down El Camino in the future. Those are the places on my mind next.

Have a blessed week. The academic semester is finally over which means I have time on my weekends in summer to write things outside of all the notes and papers I have been. Lots of writing of all kinds this summer, thank you for reading.
